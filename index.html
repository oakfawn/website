Our Process

How We Work

1. Project Consultation & Scope Development

We meet on-site, understand your goals, and define a clear, honest scope before anything is designed.

2. Detailed Planning, Permitting & Fabrication

Drawings, permitting, and material selection move in parallel, so fabrication can start the moment we break ground.

3. On-Site Execution, Trade Oversight & Final Quality Walkthrough

Our team manages every trade on-site, keeping the schedule tight and finishing with a full walkthrough before handover.
