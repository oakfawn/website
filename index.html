Our Philosophy

Two crafts. One standard.

Oak & Fawn started from a simple frustration: it's hard to find people who build like they mean it. Contractors who show up when they say they will. Carpenters who care about a reveal line nobody else will notice. So we built a company around that kind of care — one side raising additions and remodeling homes, the other shaping wood into cabinetry and built-ins — both held to the same quiet standard: do it right, explain it clearly, and never rush the parts that matter.

A note on how we build

We hand-select hardwoods for grain and character, not just cost. Every joint is cut for strength, not just appearance — because a drawer should still glide smoothly in ten years. Finishes are chosen for how they'll wear, not just how they'll look on day one.

  • Hand-selected hardwoods, matched for grain
  • Traditional joinery built for decades of use
